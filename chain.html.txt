    <h1>Commissioner-Led Authority Structure</h1>
    <p>
      Authority flows downward through the Chain of Command and is enforced at every level.
      Members will address concerns at the lowest appropriate supervisory level unless OPR involvement is required.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="section">
    <h2>Chain of Command</h2>
    <ul>
      <li><b>Commissioner</b> — Highest authority within FHP</li>
      <li><b>Deputy Commissioner</b> — Second-in-command</li>
      <li><b>Assistant Commissioner</b> — Oversees divisions and administration</li>
      <li><b>Colonel</b> — Department Commander</li>
      <li><b>Lieutenant Colonel</b> — Deputy Commander</li>
      <li><b>Major</b> — Troop Commander</li>
      <li><b>Captain</b> — District / Area Commander</li>
      <li><b>Lieutenant</b> — Shift Commander</li>
      <li><b>First Class Sergeant</b> — Senior supervisory Sergeant</li>
      <li><b>Sergeant</b> — First-line road supervisor</li>
      <li><b>Lance Corporal</b> — Junior supervisory role</li>
      <li><b>Corporal</b> — Field leader / FTO</li>
      <li><b>Master Trooper</b> — Senior expert Trooper</li>
      <li><b>Senior Trooper</b> — Experienced Trooper</li>
      <li><b>Trooper First Class</b> — Fully certified Trooper</li>
      <li><b>Probationary Trooper</b> — Post-academy evaluation status</li>
      <li><b>Recruit Trooper</b> — Academy member</li>
    </ul>
  </div>

  <div class="section">
    <h2>Internal Affairs</h2>
    <p>
      The <b>Office of Professional Responsibility (OPR)</b> handles misconduct investigations, policy violations,
      and internal complaints. Members may report directly to OPR when required.
    </p>
  </div>
